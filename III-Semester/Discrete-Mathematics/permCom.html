05 · Four Cases

The four cases, side by side

Case 1 — Permutation (no repetition)

Pick 3 different balls from 5, and line them up. Every new order counts as a new arrangement — this is exactly the "Order Matters" demo above.

5P3 = 5×4×3 = 60

Scroll back up to the Permutation demo to replay it, or use the Experiment sliders above.

Case 2 — Combination (no repetition)

Pick 3 different balls from 5 — but this time only the group matters, not the order. This is the "Order Does Not Matter" demo above.

5C3 = 10

Scroll back up to the Combination demo to replay it, or use the Experiment sliders above.

Case 3 — Combination with repetition

Select 3 balls. You may choose the same colour more than once — and order still doesn't matter.

Your selection: 0 / 3

3 types of balls, choose 3, repetition allowed:
(n+r−1)Cr = 5C3 = 10

Case 4 — Permutation with repetition

Choose a ball colour for each of 3 positions. Repetition is allowed, and order matters — position 1, 2 and 3 are all distinct slots.
