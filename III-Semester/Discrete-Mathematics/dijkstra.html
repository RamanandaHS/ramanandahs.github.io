    Dijkstra's Algorithm – Pseudocode
    1. Set distance[source] = 0.
    2. Set all other distances to ∞.
    3. Choose the unvisited vertex with minimum tentative distance.
    4. Mark it as visited/finalized.
    5. Relax all its unvisited neighbours.
    6. Repeat until the destination is finalized or all reachable vertices are processed.
    7. Follow the previous pointers backward to reconstruct the shortest path.
