About Me

I am a Professor of Mathematics at St Joseph Engineering College, Mangaluru, Karnataka, with a keen interest in making mathematics meaningful, visual and engaging for students.

Teaching

My teaching interests include Engineering Mathematics, Discrete Mathematics, Linear Algebra, Statistics,Probability and Statistics Numerical Methods and related areas.

Research Interests

My research interests include Algebra, Lattice Theory, Dihedral Groups, Concept Lattices, Algebraic Graph Theory, Engineering Education Research and related areas.

My Approach to Mathematics Education

I believe that students should not only learn mathematical formulas and procedures, but should also be able to visualize, experiment with and understand mathematical ideas.

Through this website, I hope to share interactive tools, teaching ideas, mathematical resources and research.
