My Teaching Philosophy

Mathematics becomes more meaningful when students are able to visualize concepts, experiment with ideas and connect mathematical theory with real-world applications.

I try to use technology, visualization, computational tools, collaborative learning and problem-based activities to make mathematics more engaging.

Areas of Teaching

Engineering Mathematics

Calculus, Differential Equations, Numerical Methods, Linear Algebra and Transform Techniques.

Discrete Mathematics

Mathematical Logic, Set Theory, Combinatorics, Relations, Functions and Graph Theory.

Statistics

Correlation, Regression, Probability, ANOVA and Statistical Data Analysis.

Computational Mathematics

Python, MATLAB and computational approaches for solving and visualizing mathematical problems.
