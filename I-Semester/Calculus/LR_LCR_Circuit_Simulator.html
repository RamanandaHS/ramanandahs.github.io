Observe and Learn

    Why does current not increase instantaneously in an LR circuit?A growing current changes the magnetic flux, which induces an emf (L di/dt) opposing the change. The inductor therefore resists sudden change, and i(0) = 0 must continue smoothly.
    What is the physical meaning of the time constant?τ = L/R is the time in which the current covers 63.2% of the gap to its final value. After about 5τ the circuit is practically at steady state.
    Why does resistance control damping in an LCR circuit?Resistance turns electrical energy into heat (i²R). Energy exchanged between L and C is lost on every swing; larger R removes it faster and the oscillations die out sooner.
    Underdamped versus overdamped?Underdamped (R² < 4L/C): complex roots, current oscillates while decaying. Overdamped (R² > 4L/C): real roots, current rises and decays slowly with no oscillation. Critical damping is the borderline.
    What happens when inductance increases?In LR the growth slows (larger τ) but the final current E/R is unchanged. In LCR the natural frequency 1/√(LC) falls, so oscillations become slower.
